My Experience at the Entrepreneur Future Summit 2026, Pune

3 July 2026  ·  Business, Technology

Recently, I had the opportunity to attend the Entrepreneur Future Summit 2026, held on June 12-13 at the Radisson Blu in Hinjawadi, Pune. It was an incredible two days packed with insights, networking, and forward-looking discussions about the future of business and technology.

Here is a recap of my experience and my biggest takeaways from the event.

Day 1: Diving Deep into AI and Automation

The energy on the first day was palpable. The agenda was heavily focused on the practical applications of Artificial Intelligence, automation, and building scalable business frameworks.

Tip: One of the most recurring themes was that AI is no longer just a buzzword; it's a foundational tool for scaling modern startups.

It was fascinating to see how operators and tech leaders are implementing these technologies on the ground. For me, several sessions really stood out:

  • Sudhir Sharma's "Building Commercially Impactful Design" gave incredible perspective on how design choices directly drive business outcomes and user engagement.
  • Shreya Mahendru's "Multi-Million Remote Business Framework" was an eye-opener on structuring distributed teams for massive scale without losing agility.
  • Kiran Babu's "GTM in Agentic World" felt incredibly timely, highlighting how go-to-market strategies need to evolve as AI agents become more autonomous.

I realized that for my startup, Homeville, incorporating these insights—especially around go-to-market strategies in an AI-driven landscape and focusing on commercially impactful design—could be a game-changer for our product trajectory. We've been thinking about this closely as I recently explored building a shared brain for AI agents.

Day 2: Global Perspectives and Networking

The second day was branded as "Global Entrepreneur Day," and it lived up to the name. The speaker lineup featured an impressive mix of founders, investors, and AI leaders not just from India, but from the UAE, Estonia, Italy, and Southeast Asia.

Several insightful sessions defined my Day 2 experience:

  • Gireendra Kasmalkar broke down the nuances of "How to Raise funds from institutional investor", offering practical steps for founders looking to secure serious capital.
  • Aditi Sinha complemented this perfectly with her talk on "Raising your first $5 million", a must-attend for early-stage ventures.
  • Khushi Lunkad's "User Retention for a Billion Users" provided a masterclass on scaling products sustainably.
  • Sowjanya Bonda challenged the status quo with "Rewiring How Businesses Think", pushing us to rethink core operational models.
  • Vikram Pandya's "Buyers without heartbeat" was a fascinating dive into the future of commerce and AI-driven transactions.

Some of my key networking highlights included:

  • Meeting Aashish Pahwa: We had a great discussion diving into AI agents and Moltbook.
  • Connecting with Kiran Babu: We had an insightful conversation regarding the realities and hurdles of enterprise AI adoption.

Final Thoughts and Takeaways

Attending the Entrepreneur Future Summit was a fantastic experience that left me feeling energized and full of new ideas. The intersection of global insights with actionable AI frameworks made it highly relevant to the current startup landscape.

If you are a founder or tech leader, I highly recommend keeping an eye out for next year's summit. The connections made and the knowledge shared are truly invaluable.
